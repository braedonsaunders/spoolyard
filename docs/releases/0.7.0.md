# Spoolyard 0.7.0

Field feedback on busy drawings, sheets and specifications. Sheets read cleaner, drafting steps match the visible grid, a drawing can be split onto more sheets, and specifications you refine are kept for every new drawing.

## Drawings
- **Size callouts are lettered once per size and wherever the size changes.** A row of like branches is no longer lettered `1 1/4" NS` on every branch and `3"X1 1/4" NS` at every olet. An olet carries only its boxed item number. A vent or drain made only of fittings is still sized at its olet, and the material list gives every size. To letter every branch and olet as before, choose Drawing setup › Size callouts › Every branch and olet.
- **A busy drawing gets a sheet to itself.** It fits beside its material list but would crowd there, so the list moves to the next sheet. This applies when its items need more than half the paper the list leaves, and the sheet alone gives at least 30% more room.
- **Breaks onto other sheets are lettered automatically.** The break is lettered `CONT'D ON DWG# …` with the other sheet's drawing number, and the next sheet's first parts are drawn thin beyond the break as before. A drafter's own continuation text wins. Turn the lettering off in Drawing setup.
- **Off-sheet connector symbols** (left, right and both ways) are now the standard flag rather than small arrowheads on the pipe.

## Sheets
- **New sheet** (the Sheets group, formerly Spools) starts the next spool on a blank sheet of its own.
- **Split sheet** breaks a line onto a new sheet. Click a point on a pipe, where a weld goes in, or click a weld, flange or fitting. The side routed on goes to the new sheet. The same command is on the right-click menu as Continue on a new sheet here.
- The sheet list reads `Sheet 2 · SP-002`.

## Drafting and editing
- **New drawings are in feet and inches** on tabloid paper. The imperial templates come first, and the metric ones remain.
- **The pointer steps one paper cell at a time on not-to-scale paper.** Each cell of the isometric paper is one grid block of pipe (1'-0" by default), so a pick no longer jumps a third of the sheet. The block is the drawing's routing scale and is saved with it (Drawing setup › Grid, scale & PDF views › One paper cell routes, or Block in the status bar). Switching the units moves a default block to the new units' default.
- **To-scale sheets** can be held at a chosen standard scale (Drawing setup › Drawing scale), such as 1/4" = 1'-0" or 1:50. Unset, the sheet uses the largest standard scale that fits.
- **Placing a component no longer starts a pipe.** A tee or olet becomes the routing start, so P routes its branch.
- **Valves, couplings and unions can close a line.** Drop one on an open pipe end and it sits there with no pipe after it. The pipe keeps its cut, and a flanged outlet takes no mating flange.
- **Esc puts the command down in one press**, as in AutoCAD: Pipe mode, an armed component, Move, Copy or Measure. A second Esc clears the selection. Enter still finishes a run and stays in Pipe.
- **Paste places copies where you pick.** Copy and Ctrl+V take a base point, a typed offset, or Enter to paste beside the original. A branch copied with its olet goes onto the pipe it is pasted on, in that pipe's spool.

## Specifications
- **Your own specification library.** Save a specification to your library on its Specifications tab. New drawings then start from your copy in place of the standard one. The Specifications tab says when the drawing's copy differs from the library's, and can update either one. The home screen lists, exports and removes saved specifications. The library is kept on the device in the browser and the desktop app. Inside BidWright it is the organisation's and is shared by everyone in it.
- **A pipe moved to another specification takes its fittings with it.** Routed fittings are chosen again, and outlets and in-line components take the new specification's row for the same component and size, else the standard part. Previously a half coupling stayed on spec A's row after its header became SS-A. A component whose row comes from another specification is now shown as such in its properties.
- **Size gaps are interpolated.** Several sizes have no published standard dimension and were missing from a specification's series, including 1 1/4" threaded and socket-weld ball valves in every library specification, and 3 1/2", 5" and 22" items. Their dimensions are now interpolated between the neighbouring sizes and reported as calculated, so these pipes no longer show `CUT TO (PENDING)`.
- **The standard library is the general-service set:** carbon steel A, B and C (Class 150, 300 and 600) and stainless steel SS-A to SS-D (Class 150 to 900, now named General service). The A1 to D1 "Sweet Service" family is removed. It duplicated those classes with one operator's design conditions and mixed pipe grades. Drawings that use it keep their embedded copy.

## BidWright
- The embedded editor offers the organisation's specification library through the host (see the README's embedding table: `spoolyard:capabilities`, `spoolyard:library`, `spoolyard:library-result`).

Validation: 571 automated tests and TypeScript checking. In the private regression set of 39 issued drawings, every BOM row and cut length is unchanged, and so are all 284 audited dimensions. With its sizes set to every branch, as issued, its size lettering is unchanged too.
