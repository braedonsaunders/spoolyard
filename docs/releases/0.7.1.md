# Spoolyard 0.7.1

Fixes from a fitter's first drawings in 0.7.0.

## Editing
- **Choosing another catalogue row for a reducer, tee or olet resizes its pipes.** Choosing 4"×2" in place of 4"×3" now redraws and recuts the line at 2", up to the next reducer or branch. Before, only the row changed and the drawing stayed the same.
- **A flange, blind, cap or plug clicked near an open end goes on the end.** A click within a fifth of the pipe (or 36 px) of an open end is placed on the end. It used to become a flange pair with a stub of pipe beyond it. The hover preview shows where it goes.
- **A valve's open outlet can be closed.** Click the outlet of a valve placed on an open end with a plug, cap, blind or flange.
- **Fittings can be welded straight together.** Drag an elbow up against a tee and it stops at their makeup (both takeouts and the root gap), with no pipe cut between them. Drag it apart and it is pipe again. The pipe's properties have the same switch: No pipe: fittings welded together.
- **Mating flanges for a flanged valve** (a butterfly, say) are chosen in the Insert panel before placing: the specification's, another type such as weld neck or slip-on, or not supplied. After placing, Flanged ends sets both ends at once or each on its own. Each choice names the flange the specification would use.
- **Any dimension can be typed in place.** Double-click it, including one from a valve face to an elbow or an overall run, and type the length. The second point moves along its straight. Dimension lettering has a larger target.
- **Selecting a valve highlights the valve only, not its callouts.** Each callout (item number, F/B/G joint tag, operator) is picked and moved on its own.

## Specifications
- **Schedules show by size.** A specification that buys small bore heavier than its header schedule says so: SS-A reads `SCH 80S 1/2"–2" · SCH 10S 2 1/2"–24"`. The schedule also shows by each size in the NPS list and in a pipe's properties. SS-A's data is unchanged: its 2" and smaller pipe was always SCH 80S.

## Reliability
- **"Failed to fetch dynamically imported module"** appeared when a page left open across an update (or a cached page on GitHub Pages) tried to load a part of Spoolyard the update had replaced, such as the PDF exporter or the 3D review. Spoolyard now keeps the open drawing and reloads onto the new version. BidWright also keeps the previous release's files for pages still open.
- **The 3D review opens on any machine.** Where WebGL is not available, it opens a simplified canvas view with orbit, pan and zoom instead of failing. The 3D review on GitHub Pages most likely failed for the same stale-file reason. An error now says what actually went wrong, rather than always blaming hardware acceleration.

Validation: 576 automated tests and TypeScript checking. In the private regression set of 39 issued drawings, the BOMs, cut lengths and all 284 audited dimensions are unchanged.
