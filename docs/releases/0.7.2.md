# Spoolyard 0.7.2

More fixes from field users.

## 45° elbows
- **45° picks (F10, the 45° button in the status bar).** With Ortho on, picks keep to the routing plane (Plan, or an elevation) and can also go at 45° between its two axes. The pipe turns through 45° elbows, and its travel along each axis steps by whole grid blocks. On isometric paper a 45° in plan lies on another axis's line (north-east is straight up the page), so the plane says which is meant. Type `U` or `D` and a length for a riser.
- **Typed 45s.** `NE 4'`, `NW`, `SE` and `SW` route that length at 45° in plan.
- **45° offset.** The 45° elbows now appear in Insert. Choose one, set the offset and the direction it goes, then click a pipe. Two 45° elbows roll in an offset there. The line beyond keeps its length along the run and moves across by the offset.

## Routing
- **The East/North/Up buttons route from what is selected.** A selected end or fitting routes from itself, and a selected pipe from its open end. Before, they routed from the last end drawn, which could be off to one side, and a selected pipe gave "A pipe already runs that way". Pointing at a button marks the end it will route from. With no Length typed, it routes 10'.
- **The routing preparation stays as chosen.** Socket weld set in the ribbon used to fall back to the spec's default after the first pipe, mixing a socket-weld elbow with butt-weld ones. It now holds until you change it, the size or the specification. A pipe end prepared differently from its fitting's ends (a socket-weld pipe into a butt-weld elbow) is reported.

## Measure
- **True distance.** Measure gives the straight-line distance between its two points; it no longer locks to one axis after the first click.
- **Face snaps.** Measure snaps to where each pipe meets its fittings, the cut ends, and to flanged components' faces, so face-to-face and end-to-end can be measured.
- **Not-to-scale sheets.** Open paper between pipes has no true position, so Measure asks for a point on the work instead of returning a meaningless number.

## Cut lengths
- **How each cut is worked out** is shown in the pipe's properties: centre to centre, less each fitting's takeout and each weld gap (root gap or socket pull-back), plus any field-fit makeup, equals the cut.
- **Project profile › Weld gaps in cuts.** Deduct (default) takes the spec's root gap and socket pull-back off the cut. Ignore cuts to the fitting takeouts only, for a shop that takes up the gap at fit-up.

## 3D review
- **Flanges are solid**, with their B16.5 diameter and thickness, a raised face, and a weld-neck hub (or a slip-on collar) down to the pipe. Both flanges of a pair show with the gasket between them.
- **Flanged valves** show their own end flanges and the mating flanges.
- **Couplings and unions** are drawn as sleeves, without a handwheel.
- **Equipment** a line bolts to is shown faint.

## Continuation notes
- **Delete a continuation note** with Delete on the note, Delete note on its right-click menu, or Remove note in its properties. Ctrl+Z brings it back.
- **One automatic CONT'D ON note** at a sheet break can be left off from its properties.

Validation: 582 automated tests and TypeScript checking. The bundled sample and the field drawing behind 0.7.0 give the same BOMs, cut lengths and sheet lettering as 0.7.1. The private 39-sheet regression set was not available this time, so it was not re-run.
