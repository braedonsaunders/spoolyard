# Spoolyard 0.7.3

Shop conventions, more 45s, and sanitary tube, from field answers.

## Cut lengths: the shop's conventions
- **Project profile › Butt-weld root gap in cuts** now has *Heavy carbon only*: the root gap comes off the cut on carbon steel Sch 80 and heavier (XS, XXS), where the shop leaves one, and not on standard-wall carbon or stainless, which are fitted tight. *Deduct* and *Ignore* are as before.
- **Socket pull-back.** Set how far the pipe stops short of the socket bottom, for every socket weld in the drawing. Left empty, each specification's socket gap is used. A shop that adds 1/8" to the book centre-to-bottom enters 1/8"; 0 bottoms the pipe.
- **Use for new drawings.** Keep a project profile as the default, so every new drawing starts with the shop's conventions. *Clear default* goes back to the standard one.

## 45° and 90° elbows
- **On an open pipe end.** Choose a 45° or 90° elbow in Insert, set Toward, and click an open end. The elbow turns the line there with its outlet left open: the natural end of a spool, or where the next pipe is routed on later. Routing on straight from the outlet makes one pipe from the elbow.
- **Rolling offsets.** With a 45° or 90° elbow armed, enter Offset and Toward, and also a Roll and Roll toward: the line moves across both at once. Through 90s the line steps straight across.
- **Laterals in Insert.** Choose a lateral, set Branch toward, and click a pipe. The 45° branch leans forward along the pipe and is left open to route on from.
- **Vertical 45s.** With 45° picking (F10) on, picks can also go at 45° up or down out of the routing plane where that direction reads differently on the page from the plane's own.

## Sanitary tube (SAN-A)
- **A new standard specification:** 316L sanitary tube, ASTM A270, ½" to 6" OD, mostly orbital-welded spools with tri-clamp joints at equipment and line breaks.
- **Sized by outside diameter.** Sizes read `1.5" OD` in the size lists, BOM and properties, and pipes are called out `1.5" OD TUBE` on the sheet.
- **Fittings:** 90° and 45° tangent elbows, equal and reducing tees, concentric and eccentric reducers and end caps, with Steel & O'Brien orbital-weld dimensions. Orbital butt welds take no root gap.
- **Clamp joints** are put in like flanges: a ferrule at an open end, or a pair at a line break. Each joint is listed as one ferrule per side, one gasket and one clamp. The gasket seats in the ferrule grooves, so the joint adds no length to the cut.
- Not yet included: sanitary valves and clamp fittings (from the CFF catalogue), laterals, and fitting weights. BPE and 3-A dimension differences are still to confirm; the dimensions are the Steel & O'Brien orbital series.
