# Spoolyard 0.3.0

Production isometrics. New drawings come out as issued-for-construction sheets: CAD lettering, a bill of materials that lists every flange, gasket and bolt, flanged-joint callouts, plant coordinates, and a not-to-scale layout sized by what is on each straight. This release also fixes every item from the first round of field feedback.

## Field feedback

- **Gaskets missing from the list of materials.** Every flanged joint now produces its mating flange (or lap-joint flange and stub end), gasket and bolting in the BOM, the materials table, CSV and PCF. Identical items merge into one row. A joint whose gasket or bolts are supplied by others is called out on the sheet without adding them to the list.
- **Bolts not calculated properly.** Bolting comes from ASME B16.5 for the joint's size and class, with stud lengths that allow for both flanges, the gasket and the joint type (raised face, lap joint, wafer and lug valves). Lug valves get two sets of lug bolts. Rows are listed by diameter, then length, as `5/8"X3 1/4"`.
- **"Fabrication checks" wording, and lengths typed in for standard parts.** Standard flanges, valves, tees, reducers, caps and stub ends take their takeouts from built-in ASME B16.9/B16.10/B16.11/B16.5 and MSS SP-43 tables, so a flanged ball valve or tee no longer asks for a length. The few checks that remain name the fitting and say what to do.
- **"Select the branch pipe for each tee."** Tee branches are found from the geometry, so this check no longer appears.
- **Having to pick Pan every time to move.** Dragging empty canvas pans with any tool, and the scroll wheel zooms.
- **Percentages wrong for a fitting's position on the pipe.** A component's position is the distance and percent along the whole straight it sits on, end to end, not between its neighbours. Typing 40 % puts it at 40 % of the straight.
- **Lap-joint flanges and other fittings missing.** Added lap-joint flanges (raised and flat face) with long and short stub ends, plus lug and wafer butterfly valves. The library has 174 standard components.
- **Cut distances overlapping when things are close together.** Every label, tag and dimension is placed against the shapes already on the sheet so none overlap. Dimension and leader lines break around lettering. On not-to-scale sheets each straight is drawn with the room its valves, flanged joints and hangers need, rather than in proportion to its length.
- **Flanges can't be placed on the end of the pipe.** Dropping a flange (or any one-port component) on an open pipe end makes it the end. Flanges can also be slid to either end of a straight.

## Production sheets

- Sheet frame modelled on a real issued set: north arrow, BOM with ID/QTY/ND/DESCRIPTION and `CUT TO` lengths and `(TOTAL OF N PIECES)`, notes, reference drawings, design conditions, logos, revision block and title block. Edit it all in **Drawing setup**, or click the title block on the sheet.
- Long bills of materials stay in one table. The QTY and ND columns narrow before the table splits.
- CAD lettering (ROMANS-style stroke font with width factors) in the editor, PDF, SVG, DXF and DWG. Issued PDFs print in black, without graph paper on not-to-scale sheets.
- Flanged-joint callouts `F# B# G#` with the bolt item boxed, as on issued sheets. Boxed BOM tags with leaders, nominal-size labels, and line numbers along the pipe. Olets show their size and type, e.g. `3"X1/2" NS SOL`.
- Continuation notes (`CONT'D ON / FROM`, `CONNECT TO`, `CLOSED`) with E/N(S)/EL plant coordinates, centreline elevation callouts, tie-point hexagons, valve operators and tags, hangers, U-bolts and shoes. All of these are set in the inspector.
- Dimensions follow the drafted conventions:
  - text lies in the isometric plane;
  - flanged valves are measured face to face across their gaskets;
  - olets and located supports get their own tier next to the pipe, supports measured from the fitting on their own piece;
  - overall dimensions stop at tees;
  - screwed and fitting-to-fitting assemblies are measured only at their pipe ends and branches;
  - offsets are drawn as hatched triangles (rolling offsets as two), with angle arcs, leg lengths and the true length.
- Takeouts are shown to the nearest 1/16″.

## Validation

- 142 automated tests pass, covering joints, bolting, BOM rows, placement along a straight, PCF round trips, sheet layout and every output format. DXF output audits clean in ezdxf.
- Five sheets of a real 39-sheet issued isometric set were rebuilt in Spoolyard and compared side by side with the originals. On three, the bill of materials matches row for row: IDs, quantities, sizes, descriptions and cut lengths. On the other two it lists the same items in a different order. One of those originals also miscounts its own flanges and lug bolts, which Spoolyard does not reproduce. The drawings follow the issued dimensioning, tagging and callout conventions.
- The field-feedback checks above pass in a browser: inserting a flanged valve lists two gaskets and eight `5/8"X3 1/4"` studs with no length prompts or checks, an end flange adds its gasket and four studs, the valve reads 40 % of its straight, dragging the canvas pans in Select, and the drawing survives a reload.

Desktop installers remain unsigned.
