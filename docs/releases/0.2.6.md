# Spoolyard 0.2.6

- Place components at the point clicked on the pipe, with a preview of the target and insertion position. Selecting a component always waits for placement instead of silently inserting it at the midpoint.
- Resolve placement against the measured pipe centreline, including positions covered by connection targets or gaps behind dimension labels. Hit tolerance stays usable at different zoom levels.
- Slide inline fittings and attached components along their pipe by dragging, or enter an exact distance or percentage in the inspector. Outer pipe ends, catalogue data, end preparations and reducer size transitions are preserved.
- Keep supports and annotations associated with the correct pipe segment when a fitting moves past them.
- Show feedback for missed clicks and invalid positions while keeping the selected component available to retry. Reject positions that consume the remaining pipe after known fitting takeouts and root gaps.

Validation: 78 automated tests, including all 113 component types offered by Insert. Chrome passed all 142 palette choices in both Paper and Model space (284 placements plus repositioning and undo), as well as dragging, exact-distance edits, connection-target clicks, missed-click recovery and reload persistence. Coverage also includes zoomed hit testing, pipe-label gaps, fixed outer connections, attachments and reducer size transitions.

The published Windows x64 installer was verified by SHA-256 and installed on the work computer running Windows 11. The installed app and the live site in Microsoft Edge each passed eight representative palette choices in both Paper and Model space, plus dragging, exact-distance edits, invalid-position rejection, undo, missed-click recovery and reload persistence, with no renderer errors. Windows, macOS and Linux release builds and the production web deployment all passed.

Desktop installers remain unsigned.
