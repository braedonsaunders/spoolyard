# Spoolyard 0.6.1

Engineering values follow the same order Plant 3D uses: specification and catalogue data first, then a published standard, then a documented general calculation the specification can override. A calculated value shows its source in the properties panel and raises no warning.

- **Lug and tapped-body bolting:** a matching lug bolt row in the specification always wins. With no row, the length is flange thickness (plus the stub-end lap) + gasket + thread engagement (one bolt diameter by default, at most half the lug body) + projection (a heavy nut plus two threads), rounded up to 1/4″ (5 mm). Engagement and projection are specification parameters. Cap screws take no nut. The lengths previously fitted to one drawing set are removed.
- **Slip-on flange set-back:** the flange's catalogue row (`setBack`) wins, then the specification's `slipOnSetBack`. Otherwise it is the lesser of pipe wall and 1/4″ (6 mm), the face fillet weld size of ASME B31.3 Fig. 328.5.2B, plus an optional `slipOnClearance` (default 0).
- **3D elbows:** takeouts come from the ASME B16.9 3D tables (NPS 3/4–48, US-customary and SI). Outside the tables they are R·tan(θ/2) with R = 3 × NPS.
- **Cut schedule and sheet dimensions** now share one set of measuring stations, drafted dimensions included, so the cut schedule's overall lengths always match the sheet.
- **New settings:**
  - Project profile: the stock-length tolerance for uncut pipe (default 1/32″ / 0.8 mm).
  - Drawing setup: where a valve's gaskets are dimensioned (in the valve span or the pipe spans), and whether a flange pair on a fitting puts its gasket in the pipe span or in the fitting make-up.
- **Warnings** now appear only for missing or ambiguous data, such as an unknown pressure class, a missing gasket, or a valve pattern or butterfly category whose lengths differ. Values taken from standard tables or calculated no longer raise them.
- **Performance tests** scale their timing budgets to the machine and retry a measurement during a burst of load, so a busy runner no longer fails a release.

Validation: 562 automated tests and TypeScript checking. A private regression set of 39 issued drawings keeps all 284 audited dimensions and its BOMs unchanged.
